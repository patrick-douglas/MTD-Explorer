S8A. Reported HAllA association blocks

S8B. Individual pairs inside reported HAllA blocks
Original signed Spearman coefficients, asymptotic p values and globally BH-adjusted p values for all 49 pairs within the 24 reported blocks. Forty-five pairs are individually significant: 37 positive and eight negative. BH correction used the entire family of 425 × 20,799 = 8,839,575 taxon–gene tests, rather than this selected subset.
Note: The evaluated vectorized Spearman path used two-sided asymptotic Student-t p values, bypassing the generic configuration of 1,000 permutation iterations.
Note: Six pairs have exact rho=1 and numerically reported p=0. They share tied baseline profiles in 13 of 15 libraries, with only infected_2 and infected_3 differing. These sparse patterns do not establish a validated lncRNA hub or within-group association.
Note: Taxon names are sequence-based classifications. Association signs are separate from infected-versus-uninfected expression-change signs.
Sources: S8-HALLA-OUTPUTS, S8-HALLA-PATCH.

S8C. Expression and annotation context of HAllA-associated genes
Expression and annotation context for all 35 host genes represented in the reported HAllA blocks. Twenty-nine are protein coding and six are lncRNAs. NCBI records are active RefSeq MODEL records with an exact BGLAX cross-reference and reference-coordinate match; 17 have informative predicted product descriptions, including four of the eight DE genes.

Note: The eggNOG file contains records for 22 of 29 coding genes and six of eight DE genes, and none for the six lncRNAs. Not available denotes an absent record; Not assigned denotes a missing field in an existing record.
Note: BGLAX_044265, the lncRNA in the largest block, is uncharacterized and not DE for this contrast (adjusted p approximately 0.197).
