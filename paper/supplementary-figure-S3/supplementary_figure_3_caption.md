**Supplementary Figure S3. Runtime and resource profile of the MTD Explorer reference execution.** One local run processed 15 paired-end *Biomphalaria glabrata* RNA-seq libraries from PRJNA1306560 using Magic-BLAST and 20 requested threads, on an Intel Core i9-10900K system with 10 physical cores, 20 logical CPUs and 125.64 GiB of operating-system-visible memory. Total wall time was 54 h 58 min 15 s. **(A)** Durations of the seven recorded pipeline intervals that each accounted for at least 1% of wall time; the other nine intervals are pooled. Percentages use total wall time as the denominator. These are intervals between pipeline events, encompassing all work within each block. **(B)** Sampled sums of resident set size (RSS) and swap across monitored active descendant processes, shown separately. The RSS sum can count shared pages more than once. The sampled RSS peak was 117.27 GiB during the host-processing/expression interval; the swap peak was 13.92 GiB during non-host classification. **(C)** System-wide CPU activity as a percentage of total CPU capacity, including activity outside the pipeline. The horizontal dashed line marks the arithmetic mean across observations (65.7%). Shading in B and C identifies the HUMAnN, host-processing/expression and ssGSEA intervals. All 39,020 original resource observations are retained, with a configured sampling interval of 5 s and an observed mean interval of 5.072 s. The run started without reuse of heavy-stage intermediates and used pre-existing reference and database resources.
